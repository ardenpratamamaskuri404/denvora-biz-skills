# Question bank

Pick only the questions that change the scope or the quote. Don't ask everything.

## Always (any project)
- What is the goal? What should be different after this project?
- Who is it for (audience)? Who decides and approves?
- What exactly will you receive at the end (deliverables)?
- Deadline? Is it fixed (event/launch) or flexible?
- Do you already have content, branding, logo, or accounts ready?
- How many people give feedback? Who has the final word?
- Budget range? (Ask only if the user wants it; never assume.)
- Examples you like and dislike?

## Website / landing page
- Purpose: sell, collect leads, inform, portfolio?
- How many pages? Who writes the content and images?
- Needs: contact form, payment, login, blog, multiple languages?
- Domain and hosting: exist already, or should they be arranged?
- Who maintains it afterward?

## Online store / digital product
- What is sold (physical/digital/services)? How many products?
- Payment methods needed? Shipping or delivery method?
- Who manages products and orders after launch?
- Policies ready (refund, terms)?

## App / software
- Platform (web, Android, iOS)? Who are the users?
- Must-have features vs. nice-to-have? What is the smallest useful version?
- Data: what is stored? Any integrations?
- Who hosts and maintains it? Support expectations?

## Branding / design
- Brand personality, values, target market?
- Deliverables list (logo variants, guidelines, social templates)?
- Number of concepts and revision rounds?
- File formats and usage rights?

## Content / copywriting / video
- Format, length, tone, language?
- Who provides the source material or access to experts?
- Number of revisions? Who approves?
- Where will it be published?

## Red flags (flag, don't accuse)
- "It's simple, just like X" with no details
- Goal is unclear or keeps changing
- Many stakeholders, no single decision maker
- Deadline unrealistic for the described scope
- Asks for "everything included" without a list
- Wants to start before agreeing on scope
- Mentions unpaid "trial" work or exposure instead of payment
- Pressure to skip written agreement
