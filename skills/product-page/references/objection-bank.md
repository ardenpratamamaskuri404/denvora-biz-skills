# Buyer Objection Bank & Honest Responses

Use these frameworks to address natural buyer hesitations honestly without hype or deceptive claims.

## 1. Objection: "Is this suitable for beginners?"
- **Honest Framework:** Clarify the exact skill prerequisites.
- **Example Response:** "Yes, as long as you have basic familiarity with [Software/Tool]. We include a step-by-step setup guide with screenshots. However, if you have never opened [Software] before, you should first learn the basic interface."

## 2. Objection: "Can I use this for client projects?"
- **Honest Framework:** Distinguish between Single-Use (Personal) and Commercial / Extended licenses.
- **Example Response:** "The Commercial license permits you to use this template to build deliverables for multiple clients. You cannot, however, resell or redistribute the template files themselves as your own digital product."

## 3. Objection: "Will this become outdated?"
- **Honest Framework:** State what updates are included and what constitutes a major paid upgrade.
- **Example Response:** "All bug fixes and minor version updates for version 1.x are included free. If we release a completely new version 2.0 with new major capabilities, existing buyers will receive an upgrade discount."

## 4. Objection: "What if I get stuck?"
- **Honest Framework:** Clarify support channels and typical response SLA.
- **Example Response:** "You can reach us at [ISI: Email Support]. We respond to technical inquiries within 24-48 business hours on weekdays. We do not provide real-time phone support or bespoke custom development."

## 5. Objection: "What is your refund policy?"
- **Honest Framework:** Transparent digital refund policy.
- **Example Response:** "Because this is an instantly downloadable digital asset, we offer refunds within 7 days if the files are technically corrupted or incompatible with the explicitly stated requirements, and our support cannot fix the issue for you."
