# Product Page

Craft an honest, high-converting product page draft for a digital product or productized service. The goal is clarity and trust: clearly communicate what the product does, who it is for, what it does NOT do, and prevent post-purchase refund disputes.

## Rules (and why)

1. **Never invent testimonials, review scores, or fake customer statistics.** Fake social proof destroys credibility and violates consumer trust laws. If testimonials are not provided, either insert `[ISI: Testimoni asli dari pembeli]` / `[FILL IN: Real customer quote]` or omit social proof entirely.
2. **Never invent pricing, discounts, or fake countdowns.** Prices must come strictly from the user's inputs. If missing, use `[ISI: Rp ...]`.
3. **Be honest about limitations and prerequisites.** State hosting requirements, software dependencies, and who this product is **NOT** for. Honest disqualification attracts better buyers and cuts refunds.
4. **Draft only.** Does not constitute certified legal sales terms; provide standard draft disclaimers.
5. **Reply in the user's language** (natural Indonesian or English).

## Process

1. **Identify the product:** product name, category (template, SaaS, course, UI kit, automation), target audience, and primary outcome.
2. **Clarify core problem and transformation:** what painful friction does this eliminate?
3. **Map features to concrete benefits:** explain *why* each feature matters in practical daily workflow.
4. **Define prerequisites and compatibility:** required software, tech stack, skill level required.
5. **Specify who this is NOT for:** clear anti-personas to avoid bad reviews.
6. **Draft structure following `references/product-template.md`:** headline, problem, solution, features, compatibility, FAQ, pricing, and refund policy draft.

## Output format

1. **Hero Section:** Clear headline (Outcome + Audience), subheadline, primary Call-To-Action (CTA).
2. **The Problem:** 2-3 relatable friction points currently experienced by the target buyer.
3. **The Solution / What's Included:** Direct breakdown of deliverables (files, templates, modules).
4. **Key Features & Practical Value:** Table or bullet points connecting feature to real-world benefit.
5. **Technical Requirements & Prerequisites:** Hosting, software versions, dependencies.
6. **Who This Is For / Not For:** Clear qualification and disqualification criteria.
7. **Pricing & Package Options:** Transparent pricing tables with placeholders if unstated.
8. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):** 5-7 questions addressing common buyer objections (from `references/objection-bank.md`).
9. **Refund & Support Policy Draft:** Honest terms regarding digital product returns and support SLA.

## Boundaries

- Do not write scammy "get rich quick", guaranteed revenue, or miracle claims.
- Do not invent fake "500+ happy students" or "4.9/5 stars" badges without verified proof.
- Do not provide legally certified Terms of Service or Privacy Policy text.
